# Code of conduct

OSS Error Registry is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically
constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and assume good intent while still reviewing claims and code
  carefully.
- Give specific, actionable feedback about the contribution rather than the
  contributor.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, identities, and
  communication styles.
- Keep discussions focused on evidence, reproducibility, safety, and project
  scope.
- Remove private data, credentials, and identifying information from logs and
  fixtures.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope while remaining free to propose a
  focused alternative.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained disruption, and
retaliation are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates this policy. Repeated or serious violations may
result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through an available GitHub maintainer contact
channel. Do not include personal or sensitive details in a public issue. Reports
will be reviewed in context and handled as consistently and confidentially as
practical for a volunteer-maintained project.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents the project.
